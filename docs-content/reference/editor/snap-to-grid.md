The two icons just left of the zoom controls are **Snap to Grid** (the magnet) and **Show Grid**. They're independent with one exception: turning Snap on always turns Show Grid on too, so you can see the grid you just started snapping to. Turning Snap back off doesn't hide the grid again — it stays exactly as visible as you left it.

The grid you snap to isn't a fixed size — it's **10 pt** normally, **5 pt** once you're past 150% zoom, and **1 pt** past 400%, so snapping keeps getting finer as you zoom in for detail work instead of fighting you with one coarse grid at every level. Snap also changes nudging: with it on, an arrow key moves the selection 10 pt per press instead of 1 pt (`kbd:Shift+Arrow` is always 10 pt, Snap on or off).
