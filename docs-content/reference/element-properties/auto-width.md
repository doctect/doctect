---
title: Auto Width
summary: Makes a text box resize to fit its content as you type — and disables the overflow, wrap, and padding controls, which then have nothing to act on.
aliases: fit text, hug contents
keywords: auto width, autoWidth, fit, hug, resize, shrink to fit, fixed size, overflow, padding
---

**Auto width**, at the top of the Typography section, only shows for a text-only selection (the `autoWidth` field). Switch it on and the box resizes to fit whatever's typed, growing and shrinking as you edit; switch it off and it returns to a fixed size you set by dragging its handles.

Because an auto-width box always fits its own content exactly, three controls have nothing left to do and grey out while it's on: [Overflow](/docs/reference/text-overflow), Wrap, and [Padding](/docs/reference/text-padding), each with a note in the panel explaining why. If you need any of those, turn Auto width off first.

See [Typography](/docs/editor/elements-and-properties#typography).
