Submit the email sign-up form and you **won't be signed in yet** — a verification link goes to your address and the account stays dormant until you click it. There's no code to copy; the link is the whole ceremony. Clicking it confirms the address, signs you in automatically, and drops you back with a green "Email verified" banner, continuing wherever you were headed. Google sign-ups skip this step entirely (Google already vouched for the address).

Two limits are worth knowing. The link **expires after 1 hour** — take longer and you just request a fresh one, nothing is lost. And **Resend email** sends at most **one email per address every 5 minutes**: clicking resend again inside that window won't send another copy, and the one already in your inbox is still the valid one. A refused sign-in re-sends the link too (same 5-minute limit), so the recovery for a lost email is pleasantly lazy — go back to `/login`, try to sign in, and check your inbox again.
