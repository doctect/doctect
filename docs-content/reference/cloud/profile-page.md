Every [username](/docs/reference/username) is a link to a public **profile** at `/u/` followed by the handle — reached from the "by …" on any gallery card, a project's author line, or a review signature. A profile shows exactly three things: the **username**, the **join date**, and a grid of that author's **published projects**, each card a click into its project page. That's the entire list — no email, no real name, no private or unpublished work, no cloud saves.

Two consequences follow from "published only". [Unpublishing](/docs/reference/publish-and-unpublish) a project removes it from the profile along with the gallery (its reviews go out of public view with it — hidden, not deleted, so republishing brings them back). And an author who hasn't published yet shows "No published projects yet.", while a username that doesn't exist — including an **old handle after its owner renamed** — gets "User not found".

See [Author profiles](/docs/gallery/ratings-reviews-profiles#author-profiles).
